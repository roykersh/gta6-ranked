What this is

GTA 6 Ranked Hub ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the GTA 6 Ranked channel. It signs in only to the channel's own accounts (YouTube @GTA6RankedDaily, TikTok @gta6rankeddaily, Instagram @gta6rankeddaily) and reads statistics about the channel's own videos, such as view, like, comment and share counts.

Who may use it

The app is for the channel owner only. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up or connect an account.

What it does not do

The app does not post, comment, like, follow, message or change anything on any platform. It reads the channel's own data and shows it to the owner.

Use of TikTok, Instagram and YouTube data through the app follows each platform's own terms and developer policies. Access can be removed at any time from the platform's account settings.
