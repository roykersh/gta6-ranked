Privacy Policy

GTA 6 Ranked Hub. Effective 9 November 2026.

Whose data

GTA 6 Ranked Hub is used only by the owner of the GTA 6 Ranked channel, with the channel's own accounts. It does not collect personal information about viewers or any other users.

What it reads

After the owner connects the channel's account, the app reads:

  • basic account info: account id, display name and avatar;
  • account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts;
  • the channel's own videos: id, link, caption, date, duration and view, like, comment and share counts.

Where it is kept

The sign-in token and the numbers above are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Removing access

The connection can be removed at any time in the platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions). To ask for data to be deleted, email the address below; it is deleted promptly.
